❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before purchasing.

Will I receive the books instantly?

Yes. After your payment is completed on Gumroad, your PDF files are available for immediate download.

Can I print the worksheets multiple times?

Absolutely! You can print the worksheets as many times as you need for your personal or classroom use.

Which file format will I receive?

All products are delivered as high-quality PDF files that can be opened on computers, tablets and smartphones.

Are these suitable for homeschooling?

Yes. Our printable resources are designed for parents, teachers, homeschooling families and learning centres.

Can I use these resources in my classroom?

Yes. Teachers can print and use them for classroom activities, learning stations and homework.

Do I need any special software?

No. Any standard PDF reader such as Adobe Acrobat Reader can open and print the files.
